2 · Analysis engine powers the test

The model Kavach drives to attack & judge — separate from your target.

Why Kavach needs an analysis engine. Every scan is powered by an engine model that does two jobs your target can’t do for itself:
  • Adaptive attacker — generates multi-turn jailbreaks, prompt-injection and data-exfiltration attempts that adjust to how your target replies.
  • LLM-as-judge — reads each reply and decides whether the target actually complied or leaked data, catching failures simple pattern-matching misses.

Keep it separate from the target for objective results. Use your own BYOM key for full control, or the one-click GitHub Models fallback. The engine only probes and evaluates — it never needs your production data.
